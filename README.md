# 📊 Sales Analysis & Business Performance (Fast Food & Coffee Shop)

This Data Analysis Final Exam project focuses on exploring, analyzing, and visualizing transaction data from two culinary business sectors: **Fast Food Sales** and **Coffee Shop Sales**. The project applies Pivot Tables, data aggregation, and business metric visualization to evaluate sales performance, monthly trends, top-selling product categories, and consumer payment method preferences.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

- **Data Analysis & Processing:** Python (Pandas, NumPy) / Spreadsheet (Pivot Tables & Charts)
- **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn / Excel Charting
- **Working Environment:** Visual Studio Code / Jupyter Notebook / Microsoft Excel

## 📈 Analysis Summary & Key Findings

### 1. Fast Food Sales Analysis
- **Monthly Sales Trends:** Analyzed the highest total transaction amounts throughout the year to identify seasonal purchasing patterns (*Sheet A1*).
- **Best-Selling Products:** Identified food and beverage items with the highest quantities, such as *Cold Coffee*, *Sugarcane juice*, and *Panipuri* (*Sheet A2*).
- **Payment Methods:** Compared consumer preferences using *Cash*, *Online*, and other payment methods (*Sheet A3*).
- **Time of Sale:** Analyzed peak purchase times across categories like *Night*, *Afternoon*, and *Midnight* (*Sheet A4* - *Sheet A5*).

### 2. Coffee Shop Sales Analysis
- **Total Sales by Branch:** Evaluated store performance based on locations (*Lower Manhattan*, *Hell's Kitchen*, and *Astoria*) using *Total Sales* and *Transaction Qty* metrics (*Sheet B2*, *Sheet B4*, *Sheet B5*, *Sheet B8*).
- **Top Product Categories:** Analyzed sales contributions from categories such as *Coffee*, *Tea*, *Bakery*, and *Drinking Chocolate* (*Sheet B4*, *Sheet B5*, *Sheet B8*).
- **Popular Product Trends:** Highlighted specific products with high sales volumes over time, such as *Barista Espresso* and *Brewed Chai Tea* (*Sheet B9* - *Sheet B10*).
